# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual and romantic identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respect, constructive feedback, responsibility, and focus on what is best for the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, sexualized attention, threats, stalking, publishing private information, or other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through GitHub's repository reporting tools. Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban participants whose behavior is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
